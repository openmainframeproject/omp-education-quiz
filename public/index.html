Chapter 2 - JCL Basics

Understanding the JOB Statement

Every batch job on a mainframe begins with a JOB statement. It tells z/OS who is running the job, how it should be run, and what happens when it finishes...

//MYJOB JOB 'ACCT','TUSHAR',
      CLASS=A,MSGLEVEL=(1,1)
Knowledge Check

What does the JOB statement do?

✓ Starts a batch job and sets its attributes
